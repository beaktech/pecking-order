# Community code of conduct

Pecking Order welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone participating in the project's issues, pull requests and other community spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and useful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss the work and explain disagreements without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect people's privacy and their decisions about sharing recordings and locations.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, personal attacks and sharing someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated disruption or unwanted contact is also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **hello@beaktech.org**. Include the relevant links and enough context to understand what happened. Do not post private evidence or sensitive personal information in public issues.

Beak Tech's project maintainers review reports, treat sensitive information confidentially where practicable, and consider the context and impact. They may request a change in behaviour, remove content, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude someone from the project. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in immediate exclusion.

A person involved in a report should not decide its outcome. Concerns about an enforcement decision can be sent to the same contact for review by an uninvolved maintainer where one is available. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
